SYSTEM PIPELINE

Complete 12-Stage Platform Architecture

From drone flight mission to final government-endorsed cadastral records.

01 / CAPTURE
🚁 Drone Capture
Autonomous waypoint mission flying at 80m altitude for 2.4cm GSD.
02 / PROCESS
📸 Photogrammetry
Generate orthorectified imagery (ORI), DSM, and bare-earth DTM.
03 / UNDERSTAND
🤖 AI Understand
Semantic segmentation models detect roofs, roads, and land plots.
04 / EXTRACT
📍 Extract Features
Vectorize building footprints, road curbs, and compound walls.
05 / MAP
🗺️ Generate Parcel Map
Derive preliminary cadastral boundaries with unique parcel IDs.
06 / TWIN
🏙️ Build 3D Digital Twin
Extrude 3D building heights from DSM surface differential.
07 / DETECT
🔄 Detect Changes
Multi-temporal differencing against 2025 legacy master cadastre.
08 / VALIDATE
🧠 Topology Validation
Check overlapping polygons, sliver gaps, and self-intersections.
09 / FLAG
⚠️ Flag Uncertain Areas
Confidence scoring identifies low-confidence & obscured parcels.
10 / VERIFY
👨‍💻 Surveyor Verification
AI Surveyor Copilot interactive polygon vertex correction.
11 / GROUND TRUTH
📍 Ground Truthing
CORS GNSS RTK field tie-in and geotagged evidence photos.
12 / DELIVER
✅ Final GIS Output
Class-A cadastral Shapefile, GeoJSON & official audit reports.
